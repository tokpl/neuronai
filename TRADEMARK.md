# Trademark Policy — NeuronAI

**NeuronAI** (and the product title **Neuron - AI Memory**) are used to identify this project and its official distributions.

## What is open source

The **source code** of NeuronAI is released under the [Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE). You may use, modify, and distribute the software under those terms.

The Apache License **does not** grant rights to use the project’s names, logos, or brand assets as trademarks (see Apache License 2.0, Section 6).

## What remains protected

The following are **not** licensed as free brand assets under Apache-2.0:

- The name **NeuronAI**
- The phrase **Neuron - AI Memory** (as a product title / slogan)
- Official logos, wordmarks, and marketing artwork in `docs/assets/` (and equivalents)

You may refer to NeuronAI for **accurate, factual** description (for example: “compatible with NeuronAI”, “fork of NeuronAI”, “built using NeuronAI”).

## Please do not

Without prior written permission from the project maintainers:

- Use **NeuronAI** (or confusingly similar names) in a way that suggests your product is the **official** NeuronAI release, or is endorsed by the NeuronAI project
- Use the official **logo** or wordmark on your product, store listing, or marketing as if it were your own brand
- Register domains, packages, or social handles that impersonate the official project (for example presenting yourself as the primary “NeuronAI” publisher)

## Allowed use (examples)

- Saying your tool “integrates with NeuronAI” or “uses the NeuronAI MCP tools”
- Publishing a fork under a **clearly different** name, with attribution to the upstream project
- Using the name in academic or journalistic discussion

## Cloud and commercial services

The maintainers may offer optional hosted or commercial services (for example a future Cloud offering) under separate terms. Those services are **not** required to use the local open-source product.

The **local / self-hosted NeuronAI** software remains available under Apache-2.0.

## Questions

For trademark or branding permission requests, open a GitHub Discussion or contact the maintainers listed on the repository. Please do not use public issues for confidential legal matters.

This policy is intended to keep the ecosystem clear for users—not to restrict legitimate open-source collaboration.
