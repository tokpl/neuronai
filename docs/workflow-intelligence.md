# Workflow Intelligence

Neuron remembers **technical work context** — what was in progress and why — not private life or people metrics.

Package: `@neuron-ai-memory/workflow-intelligence`  
Persistence: `.neuron/workflow.json`, `.neuron/work-summary.md`, `.neuron/docs/handoff.md`

## Never stores

- private conversations
- non-project activity
- personal data
- employee monitoring / time tracking of people

See [session-memory.md](./session-memory.md) and [handoff.md](./handoff.md).
